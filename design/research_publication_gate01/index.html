I. THE QUESTION

A single benchmark score tells us how often a system succeeds. It rarely tells us where it went wrong.

We followed eighteen fixed questions through four retrieval configurations. Each question had known supporting evidence. At the final selection boundary, we recorded whether that complete evidence survived — or the stage at which it was lost.

What follows is not a claim about the quality of a model's answer. It is a closer look at the information the model would have been allowed to see.

II. WHAT THIS FIGURE MEANS

The boundary matters.

Evidence inclusion is not answer correctness. It measures whether the complete known evidence reached final context selection — not whether a language model used it faithfully.

The experimental scope

These are fixed synthetic diagnostic cases, deliberately constructed to expose distinct failure modes. Their counts describe this benchmark, not expected performance on customer data.

The measured progression

Token-aware chunking, hybrid retrieval and reranking are compared as fixed pipeline configurations. This figure shows case-level outcomes; it does not establish that each change independently causes the entire observed gain.

Figure 04 is a separate calibrated context-pressure mechanism. Figure 05 is a separately reviewed, fixed public SQuAD transfer probe: its 30-case rates cannot be pooled with the 18 synthetic cases. Neither set measures generated-answer correctness or customer-data performance.
